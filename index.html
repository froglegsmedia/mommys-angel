01 — The story

Parenting, but make it fashion.

Love, but make it loud.

There is a kind of person who sets out a breakfast bowl, checks the warmth of the blanket, and wishes the goat a happy Tuesday. Mommy’s Angel is for them.

We are making food worthy of that devotion: nutritious recipes, with lion’s mane in the calming bowls to support a sense of calm. Beside the food, a house style — bright, printed, and a little strange. A cat tree can look like a Sphynx. A coat can look like a poster. The pasture can wear color too.

Every piece on this page is a placeholder while the kitchen and the studio finish their work.

Two lines, one devotion.

Food is the heart of the house. One line is dedicated to Sphynx cats. The other covers every animal you love, with separate recipes for the living room and the barn.
