01 — Our story

For the angel
of the house

Mommy's Angel is a small atelier devoted to pets who are loved like family.

We make the things that live closest to them: a collar with a quiet charm, a bed that holds the afternoon, a knit for the first cold walk. Each piece is fitted slowly, in small batches, with the patience of couture and the ease of home.

Named for the companion who waits by the door, claims the sunniest cushion, and makes an ordinary room feel watched over.

The opening edit is still on the worktable. Leave your email, and we will write once — when it is ready to come home with you.
